Classifying Operational Incidents

The VoltifyAI model analyzes input parameters to place electrical string health into one of five distinct operational states defined in the physical dataset.

Normal

Full structural array efficiency. Voltage and current levels perform within optimal calculated parameters.

Optimal

Short-Circuit

Parallel shunt anomalies. Drastic reduction in voltage output while current stays elevated, introducing hazard risks.

Critical

Degradation

Cell aging or corrosion. Linear reduction in fill-factor leading to a compressed maximum power point curve.

Warning

Open Circuit

Wiring disconnection. Total interruption of current flow (`idc` falls to zero) while voltage floats near open limits.

Critical

Shadowing

Occlusion by debris or surrounding structures. Distorts irradiance ratio relative to measured panel temperatures.

Muted
Feature Engineering

The XGBoost Feature Vector

VoltifyAI translates 4 raw physical sensor metrics into a highly optimized 7-dimensional feature space. This engineered mapping provides the classification engine with explicit physical ratio features to maintain precision across highly volatile outdoor conditions.

1

Clipping Limits: Raw inputs are bounded against historical 1st and 99th dataset percentiles to filter out telemetry anomalies.

2

Ratio Modeling: Performance and Irradiance-to-Temperature ratios highlight transient load changes.

Voltage (vdc1)

Raw Input

Measures string voltage output. Directly indicators open circuits (maximum values) and short circuits (critical drops).

Current (idc1)

Raw Input

Tracks electrical flow rate. Collapses to 0A under open circuit, remains high during shorts, and degrades with wear.

DC Power (pdc1)

Engineered

Calculated via Vdc × Idc. Represents physical energy yield of the string and represents the primary output metric.

Irradiance (irr)

Raw Input

Measures incoming solar flux in W/m². Used as baseline factor to normalize electrical parameters.

Perf. Ratio (perf_ratio1)

Engineered

Calculated via DC Power / (Irradiance + ε). Directly tracks electrical efficiency relative to light availability.

Temp. Ratio (irr_temp)

Engineered

Calculated via Irradiance / (Panel Temperature + ε). Normalizes physical panel thermal drag against weather fluctuations.
